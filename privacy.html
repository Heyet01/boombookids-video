Privacy Policy

BoomBoo Uploader is used only by the owner of the BoomBoo Kids YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. It uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload videos, set thumbnails and update the channel's own settings.

It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users. No data is used for advertising.

Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
